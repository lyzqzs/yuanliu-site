My work begins with absence.
我的作品始于缺席。

When people leave, the relationships they formed with the world do not disappear. They remain in objects and continue to enter new relationships over time.
人离开以后，人与世界建立的关系并不会结束。它们会停留在物上，并在未来不断进入新的关系。

I am interested in finding these relationships that are still alive.
我一直在寻找这些仍然活着的关系。

I do not collect objects. I collect evidence that these relationships still exist, even if the way they exist has changed.
我不是在收集物，而是在收集这些关系仍然存在的证据，即使它们存在的方式已然改变。

The objects we touch, the spaces we inhabit, the words we write, and the photographs we exchange all preserve traces of encounters between people and the world. These traces are rediscovered, reinterpreted, and continue to form new relationships over time.
我们触碰过的物品、生活过的空间、写下的文字、交换过的照片，都保存着人与世界相遇的痕迹。它们会在之后的时间里被重新发现、重新理解，并再次进入新的关系。

Because of this, I do not see objects as containers of memory. I see them as witnesses to relationships. What draws me to an object is not what it is, but who it once belonged to, what it has been through, and how it continues to form new relationships after the person has gone.
因此，我并不把物看作承载记忆的容器，而更愿意把它们理解为关系的见证者。它们之所以吸引我，并不是因为它们是什么，而是因为它们曾经属于谁、经历过什么，又如何在人离开之后继续建立新的关系。

My work usually begins with collecting. I collect objects, writings, photographs, and stories left behind by others, allowing them to meet again and form new relationships through photography, text, artist books, and installation.
我的创作通常从收集开始。我收集别人留下的物品、文字、照片和故事，让它们重新相遇，并在摄影、文本、艺术家书和装置中形成新的联系。

I am not trying to reconstruct a complete past, because the past can never be reproduced. Instead, I hope to create situations where traces from different people and different moments encounter one another within the same space.
我并不试图还原一个完整的过去，因为过去永远无法被复制。我更希望让来自不同时间、不同人的痕迹在同一个空间里相遇。

Photography is always present in my work, but it is rarely the destination. It is a way of entering relationships—a way of approaching materials, observing traces, and creating new connections between images, objects, and texts, even when I can no longer meet those to whom they once belonged.
摄影始终存在于我的作品中，但它很少只是终点。更像是一种进入关系的方法，一种靠近材料、观察痕迹和重新建立连接的方式，让图像、物件和文字彼此不断移动，即使我已无法与它们最初的拥有者相见。

Everything I make begins with the impossibility of meeting again.
所有作品，都建立在一种无法相见之上。
